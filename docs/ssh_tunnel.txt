SSH TUNNEL — truy cap he thong tu may Mac
=========================================
Chay lenh nay TREN MAY MAC (khong phai tren workstation).
100.116.14.127 la IP Tailscale cua workstation (msi-MS-7E06).


### Lenh chinh (copy dong nay)

ssh -N -L 5173:localhost:5173 -L 8000:localhost:8000 -L 8001:localhost:8001 -L 8002:localhost:8002 msi@100.116.14.127


### Ban co keepalive (dung khi tunnel bi treo neu de lau)

ssh -N -o ServerAliveInterval=30 -o ServerAliveCountMax=3 \
    -L 5173:localhost:5173 \
    -L 8000:localhost:8000 \
    -L 8001:localhost:8001 \
    -L 8002:localhost:8002 \
    msi@100.116.14.127
